⏰ Hour by hour, and the "lid" on our air

Bars: forecast PM2.5 each hour. Line: the height of the lid (the mixing height). At night the lid comes down to about 200 m and squeezes the air into a thin box, so PM2.5 goes up. In the afternoon the sun heats the ground, the lid rises to about 800 m and the air has more room. Touch an hour to see the box.

🔥 Fires in the region

Total fire power seen by the VIIRS satellite each day (GW). More fire power means more smoke. Wind decides if the smoke comes to Singapore.
